Washington law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Fast-growing counties must plan and adopt development regulations under the GMA; every county must adopt critical-areas regulations (36.70A.060). Count of zoned counties not confirmed.
    RCW 36.70A.040 and 36.70A.060
  • Homeschool
    high
    Notice, parent qualifications, and annual testing.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Permit required; permitless only during outdoor recreation.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
    Rooftop capture needs no water right; use on site.
  • Off-grid notes
    Composting toilets allowed with health permit; some counties allow rainwater as drinking water.
  • State income tax, top rate
    None
    No wage tax; 7%-9% tax on large capital gains.
