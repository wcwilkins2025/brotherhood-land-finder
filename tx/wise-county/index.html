Rules

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Counties have no general zoning power. Chapter 231 gives narrow exceptions: Padre Island (Cameron, Willacy), Amistad area of Val Verde County, named lake areas, Falcon Lake, Lake Somerville, military zones.
