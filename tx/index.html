Texas law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Counties have no general zoning power. Chapter 231 gives narrow exceptions: Padre Island (Cameron, Willacy), Amistad area of Val Verde County, named lake areas, Falcon Lake, Lake Somerville, military zones.
    Texas Local Government Code ch. 231 (County Zoning Authority)
  • Homeschool
    none
    No notice; home schools treated as private schools.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Permitless since Sept 1, 2021; handguns; holster for open carry.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    Bars nuisance suits against operations running one year unchanged.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
    Sales-tax exempt equipment; HOAs cannot ban systems.
  • Off-grid notes
    Few codes in unincorporated areas; composting toilets legal; rainwater may be potable.
  • State income tax, top rate
    None
    No state income tax.
