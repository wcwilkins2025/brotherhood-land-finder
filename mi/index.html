Michigan law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Townships, cities, villages and counties may zone; a township with its own ordinance is exempt from county zoning (MCL 125.3209). Share of rural townships without zoning not confirmed.
    Michigan Zoning Enabling Act, MCL 125.3201 and 125.3209
  • Homeschool
    none
    Home-school option needs no notice or testing.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Resident permits only; 21+.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    NALC chart shows many provisions; broad statute.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    no data
  • State income tax, top rate
    4.25%
    Flat; local taxes excluded.
