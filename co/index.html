Colorado law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    County planning commissions have a duty to adopt a master plan (30-28-106); zoning plans are permissive unless the board orders one (30-28-111). Count not confirmed.
    Colorado Revised Statutes 2024, Title 30, 30-28-106 and 30-28-111
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Notice plus testing or evaluation in set grades.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Resident permits only; 21+.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed limited
    Two barrels, 110 gal total, outdoor use only; rural exempt-well permit option.
  • Off-grid notes
    Composting toilets allowed but septic usually still required; tiny homes on wheels treated as RVs.
  • State income tax, top rate
    4.40%
    Flat.
