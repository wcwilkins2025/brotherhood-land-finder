North Dakota law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Boards of county commissioners may regulate the location and use of buildings and land (NDCC 11-33-01); no mandate. Count of zoned counties not confirmed.
    N.D. Cent. Code ch. 11-33
  • Homeschool
    high
    Notice, parent qualifications, and testing with exceptions.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Concealed only; carry state ID and inform police; open carry needs permit.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    Constitutional right-to-farm (2012) reported; not confirmed this session.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    no data
  • State income tax, top rate
    2.50%
    Graduated; two brackets.
