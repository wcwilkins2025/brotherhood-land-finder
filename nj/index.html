New Jersey law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    The municipal governing body may adopt a zoning ordinance after the planning board adopts the master plan's land use element; counties do not zone.
    N.J.S.A. 40:55D-62 (Municipal Land Use Law)
  • Homeschool
    none
    No notice, testing, or qualifications.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Formerly may-issue; justifiable-need ended after Bruen; strict place limits.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    no data
  • State income tax, top rate
    10.75%
    Graduated.
