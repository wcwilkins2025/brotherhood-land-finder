Alaska law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    First and second class boroughs must provide areawide planning and land use regulation. Land outside organized boroughs forms one unorganized borough with no borough government.
    Alaska Stat. 29.40.010; AS 29.03.010
  • Homeschool
    none
    Homeschool statute needs no notice, testing, or qualifications.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Permitless since 2003; 21+ concealed.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed

    No state income tax.
