Massachusetts law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Zoning is defined as ordinances and bylaws adopted by cities and towns; counties play no role. Share of rural towns without zoning not confirmed.
    Mass. Gen. Laws c. 40A, s. 1A
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Notice to local school committee plus periodic assessment.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Formerly may-issue; place and purpose limits remain.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    Composting toilets allowed since 2016 with board of health approval; many towns mandate sewer.
  • State income tax, top rate
    9.00%
    Graduated; 4% surtax on income over about $1M.
