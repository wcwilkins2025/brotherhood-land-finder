  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    County legislative bodies are authorized to regulate land outside municipalities (T.C.A. 13-7-101); permissive. Count of zoned counties not confirmed.

  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Notice and parent diploma; testing only for independent option.

    permitless
    Handguns only; 18+ after 2023 settlement.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    Composting toilets barred where running water exists unless wastewater system approved.

    None
    No state income tax.
