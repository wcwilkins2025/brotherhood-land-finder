Florida law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Every county must maintain a comprehensive plan (163.3167) and must adopt land development regulations implementing it (163.3202).
    Fla. Stat. 163.3167 and 163.3202
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Home-education statute needs notice and annual evaluation; umbrella school avoids both.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Permitless since July 1, 2023; open-carry ban struck Sept 2025.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    2022: losing plaintiff pays farm's costs and fees.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
    Some counties offer rebates.
  • Off-grid notes
    Sewer connection mandatory where available; composting toilet alone not enough.
  • State income tax, top rate
    None
    No state income tax.
