Arizona law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Every county must adopt a comprehensive plan (A.R.S. 11-804); zoning is permissive ('may', A.R.S. 11-811). Count of zoned counties not confirmed.
    Arizona Revised Statutes 11-804 and 11-811
  • Homeschool
    low
    One-time affidavit to county; no testing.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Permitless since 2010; 21+ concealed.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    2021: mandatory fee award against bad-faith nuisance suits.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    Composting toilets allowed with permit; utilities add solar-owner fees (2023 source).
  • State income tax, top rate
    2.50%
    Flat.
