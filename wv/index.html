West Virginia law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    County commissions 'may' adopt a zoning ordinance (8A-7-1); a petition by 10 percent of voters forces an election on it (8A-7-7). Count of zoned counties not confirmed.
    W. Va. Code 8A-7-1 and 8A-7-7
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Notice and parent diploma; assessment depends on option.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Permitless since May 24, 2016; 18+ concealed.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    2019: plaintiff must own land within half mile; no punitive damages.

    allowed
