Oregon law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Each city and county shall adopt a comprehensive plan meeting statewide goals and enact land use regulations to implement it.
    ORS 197.175
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Notice plus testing in set grades.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Permit required; recognizes only CA, ID, NV, WA residents.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
    ORS 537.141(1)(h): rooftop capture exempt from water-right permit.
  • Off-grid notes
    Farm/forest zoning sets 20-160 acre minimum parcels; composting toilets allowed since 1978.
  • State income tax, top rate
    9.90%
    Graduated; local taxes excluded.
