Hawaii law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    The state Land Use Commission places all land in the state in urban, rural, agricultural or conservation districts (HRS 205-2); counties zone within that (HRS 46-4).
    Haw. Rev. Stat. 205-2 and 46-4
