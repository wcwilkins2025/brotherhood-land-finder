Missouri law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    County zoning may start only after voters approve the ballot question 'Shall county zoning be adopted?' (RSMo 64.845). Count of zoned counties not confirmed.
    RSMo 64.845
  • Homeschool
    none
    No notice; HSLDA lists record-keeping/assessment requirement.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Permitless since Jan 1, 2017; open carry may be locally restricted.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    Constitution Art. I Sec. 35 (2014) guarantees right to farm.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    Many rural counties have no zoning or building codes; approved septic still required.
  • State income tax, top rate
    4.70%
    Graduated; local taxes excluded.
