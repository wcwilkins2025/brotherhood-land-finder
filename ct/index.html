Connecticut law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    No county government. Any town or city may adopt the zoning chapter by vote and act through a zoning commission. Share of rural towns without zoning not confirmed.
    Conn. Gen. Stat. 8-1
  • Homeschool
    none
    No notice now; new law effective 2027 under HSLDA challenge.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Formerly may-issue; no nonresident permits.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    NALC chart shows few provisions; weaker statute.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    Towns may require sewer connection; occupancy cannot be denied for alternative power.
  • State income tax, top rate
    6.99%
    Graduated.
