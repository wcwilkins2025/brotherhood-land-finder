Vermont law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    A municipality may regulate land development through zoning bylaws in conformance with its plan; counties do not zone. Share of towns without zoning not confirmed.
    24 V.S.A. 4411
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Enrollment notice plus annual assessment.
  • Carry
    permitless
    Never required a permit; 16+.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    Composting toilets named in law; septic still likely; solar under 15 kW needs registration only.
  • State income tax, top rate
    8.75%
    Graduated.
