New York law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Town boards are empowered to zone the part of the town outside villages; counties do not zone. Share of rural towns without zoning not confirmed.
    N.Y. Town Law 261
  • Homeschool
    moderate
    Notice, mandated subjects, and annual assessment; likely rated high by HSLDA.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Formerly may-issue; NYC permits separate; many sensitive-place limits.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
    Large cisterns may need local permit.
  • Off-grid notes
    no data
  • State income tax, top rate
    10.90%
    Graduated; local taxes excluded.
