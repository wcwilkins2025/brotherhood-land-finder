  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Every county must adopt a general plan (Gov. Code 65300); zoning ordinances are permissive (65850). Count of zoned counties not confirmed.
    Cal. Gov. Code 65300 and 65850

    low
    File private school affidavit; no testing.

    shall issue
    Formerly may-issue; proper-cause ended after Bruen; no nonresident permits.

  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
    Rainwater Capture Act 2012: rooftop capture needs no water right.
