North Carolina law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Counties may zone under G.S. 160D. UNC survey: 79 of 100 counties had adopted zoning (countywide or partial) by 2012; all counties with over 50,000 unincorporated residents zone.
    UNC School of Government, 2012 Zoning Survey Report (Owens and Batten)
  • Homeschool
    high
    Notice, parent diploma, and annual standardized test.
  • Carry
    shall issue
    Permit required; nonresident permits available.
  • Right to farm
    Yes
    Updated after 2018; defense failed in Smithfield hog cases.
  • Rainwater collection
    allowed
  • Off-grid notes
    no data
  • State income tax, top rate
    3.99%
    Flat; cut in 2026.
