Ohio law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Zoning outside cities is by township (ORC 519.02); county rural zoning applies only in townships whose voters approve it (303.11). Share of unzoned townships not confirmed.
    Ohio Rev. Code 303.02, 303.11 and 519.02

    low
    Annual notice; no testing or qualifications.
