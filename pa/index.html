Pennsylvania law for homesteaders

  • County zoning, statewide pattern
    Zoning is by municipality; county zoning applies only to land in municipalities with no zoning ordinance of their own (MPC s. 602). Share of unzoned rural municipalities not confirmed.
    Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning Code (Act 247 of 1968), s. 602
  • Homeschool
    high
    Home-education program: notice, parent diploma, annual evaluation and portfolio.
  • Carry

    Resident permits only; 21+.
